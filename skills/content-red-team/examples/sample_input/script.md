# Script: PM interview prep in 7 days

Hey everyone, welcome back to the channel. In this video I am going to talk about how to prepare for a product manager interview.

First, a little about me. I have been in product for ten years and I have interviewed hundreds of candidates.

Here is the thing most people get wrong: they memorise frameworks like CIRCLES and then recite them like a robot. Interviewers can tell in thirty seconds.

So here is the seven day plan. Day one and two: understand the company and its product. Use it, break it, write down five things you would change.

Day three: practise product sense questions out loud. Record yourself. It is painful and it works.

Day four: metrics. Pick any app and define its north star metric, then three guardrail metrics. Be ready to explain why.

Day five: execution and prioritisation. Use a simple impact versus effort grid, but explain your reasoning, not the grid.

Day six: behavioural stories. Prepare four stories using situation, action, result. Include one failure.

Day seven: mock interview with a friend, then rest. Sleep matters more than one more framework.

If you follow this plan you will get an offer, guaranteed. Honestly, I have never seen it fail.
