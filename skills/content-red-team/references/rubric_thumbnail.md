# Thumbnail rubric

Dimensions: glance_legibility, emotion_focal_point, title_synergy, brand_fit, honesty.

## Look at the image, not just the text
Reviewers receive the full image plus 360px and 120px previews. Judge the way a phone feed shows it.

## What to look at
- **120px test**: is the text still readable? Is there one focal point? Count competing elements (aim for 3 or fewer).
- **Text**: 1-4 words ideal; high contrast with background; not covered by the timestamp badge (bottom-right) or UI overlays.
- **Face / emotion**: is there one? Does it match the video's tone? Is the eye line leading to the text or object?
- **Colour and contrast**: separation between subject and background; palette distinct from neighbours in the niche.
- **Composition**: safe margins; subject not cropped awkwardly; negative space.
- **Honesty**: imagery the video does not deliver is a risk.
- **Synergy**: with the title, a stranger should get two complementary pieces of information.

## Cite evidence
Name the region: "top-left headline text, ~6% of frame height, unreadable at 120px". Pixel measurements supplied by `thumb_preview.py` are measured; everything else is judgment.

## Variants
Rank them, say which pairs best with which title.
