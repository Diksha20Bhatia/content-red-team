# Title rubric

Dimensions (scored 1-10, defined in `config/rubric.yaml`): clarity, curiosity, keywords_fit, audience_fit, honesty.

## What to look at
- **Length**: mobile feeds truncate around 50-60 characters. Count them. Put the payload first.
- **Promise**: can a stranger say what they get? A specific noun/number beats an adjective.
- **Curiosity gap**: a real gap (an unanswered "why/how") beats a fake one (withholding the topic). A gap the video does not close is clickbait.
- **Keywords**: the term the audience would type or recognise. Do not stuff.
- **Capitalisation/punctuation**: ALL CAPS and stacked punctuation read as spam to some audiences and as energy to others; judge against the audience.
- **Honesty**: if the title claims a result, does the script prove it?
- **Thumbnail pairing**: title and thumbnail text should add, not repeat.

## Variants
Rank A/B/C. State which one you would ship and why, and which pair best with the thumbnails.

## Cite evidence
Quote the exact words or character count. "Too long (68 chars; cut after 'before')" is evidence; "feels long" is not.
