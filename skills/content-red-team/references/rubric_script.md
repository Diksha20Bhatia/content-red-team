# Script rubric

Dimensions: hook_first_20s, structure_pacing, value_clarity, voice_authenticity, credibility, cta_conversion.

## What to look at
- **First 20 seconds** (about 50-60 spoken words): states or implies the payoff, creates stakes, no throat-clearing, no "in this video I will".
- **Title/thumbnail promise**: is it paid off, and when? Early enough?
- **Structure**: clear beats; each section earns its place; flag sections to cut, tighten or add.
- **Retention beats**: pattern interrupts, open loops, examples every ~30-60s for long-form.
- **Voice**: sounds spoken, not written; consistent register.
- **Credibility**: every factual claim checkable; no guarantees; flag income/job/medical/legal claims.
- **CTA**: one clear ask, placed after value, relevant to the offer in the brief.
- **Format**: Shorts need a hook in ~1-2 seconds and a loopable end; webinars tolerate longer ramps; long-form needs mid-video re-hooks.

## Cite evidence
Quote the line or give a line number / timestamp estimate ("para 3", "~0:45").

## Cuts to Shorts
For long videos, nominate the 2 best self-contained moments (30-60s) with the reason each stands alone.
